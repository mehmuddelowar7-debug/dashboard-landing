Applied Analytics Project

Restaurant Analytics Dashboard

An applied analytics project designed to evaluate operational performance, pricing dynamics, and revenue patterns in a food-service context.

  • Designed KPI systems for revenue, demand, and discount tracking
  • Analyzed customer behavior and order patterns to support pricing decisions
  • Identified operational inefficiencies affecting service performance
  • Applied predictive modeling for decision-support scenarios

Key Insights

  • Revenue performance is more strongly influenced by average order value than total order count
  • Discount levels show limited impact on margins under observed conditions
  • Demand trends provide signals for staffing and operational planning
  • Geographic variation highlights opportunities for targeted growth strategies

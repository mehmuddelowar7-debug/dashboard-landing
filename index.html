Research Orientation

This work is guided by a central question: how can businesses use existing data more effectively to support decision-making? The focus is on transforming operational data into structured insights using KPI frameworks, scenario analysis, and applied predictive methods.

Applied Analytics Project

Restaurant Analytics Dashboard

An applied analytics project designed to evaluate operational performance, pricing dynamics, and revenue patterns in a food-service context.

  • Designed KPI systems for revenue, demand, and discount tracking
  • Analyzed customer behavior and order patterns
  • Identified operational inefficiencies affecting service performance
  • Applied predictive modeling for decision-support scenarios

Key Insights

  • Revenue driven more by average order value than order count
  • Discounts show limited impact on margins
  • Demand trends support staffing and operational planning
  • Geographic differences highlight targeted growth opportunities

Research Relevance

This project demonstrates how data analytics can be applied in real business environments to support decision-making. It highlights the gap between available data and its practical use in operations, particularly in SME contexts.

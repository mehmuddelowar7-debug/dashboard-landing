Applied Analytics Project

Restaurant Analytics Dashboard

An applied analytics project designed to evaluate operational performance, pricing dynamics, and revenue patterns in a food-service context.

  • Designed KPI systems for revenue, demand, and discount tracking
  • Analyzed customer behavior and order patterns
  • Identified operational inefficiencies affecting performance
  • Applied predictive modeling for decision-support scenarios

Key Insights

  • Revenue driven more by average order value than order count
  • Discounts show limited impact on margins
  • Demand patterns support operational planning
  • Geographic variation highlights growth opportunities

Research Relevance

This project demonstrates how analytics can be applied in real business environments to support decision-making. It highlights the gap between available data and its practical use in SME operations.

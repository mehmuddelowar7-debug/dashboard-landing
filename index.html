Applied Analytics Project

Restaurant Analytics Dashboard

An applied analytics project based on operational data and decision-making patterns in a food-service business environment.

  • Designed KPI systems for revenue, demand, and discount tracking
  • Analyzed customer behavior and order patterns
  • Identified operational inefficiencies affecting performance
  • Applied predictive modeling for decision-support scenarios
  • Grounded in real operational workflows and decision contexts

Key Insights

  • Revenue driven more by average order value than total order count
  • Discounts show limited impact on margins
  • Demand trends support operational planning
  • Geographic variation highlights growth opportunities

Analytical Scope

  • Revenue and order volume analysis across locations and entities
  • Customer behavior analysis including basket size and discount effects
  • Operational variables such as preparation time and delays
  • Scenario-based analysis using configurable inputs

Decision-Support & Modeling

  • Regression-based model used to estimate expected order value
  • Incorporates operational and pricing variables
  • Designed for scenario testing rather than exact forecasting
  • Supports evaluation of operational and pricing decisions

Research Relevance

This project demonstrates how analytics can be applied in real business environments to support decision-making. It highlights the gap between available data and its actual use in business operations, particularly in SME contexts.
